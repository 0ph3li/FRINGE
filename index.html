The Fringe
Method

a lesson on getting the hair you want, how you want it.

FRINGE: When did you first cut your own?
At fifteen, at two in the morning, with kitchen scissors. Everyone has that night. We just made sure the morning after is better.

FRINGE: What does bleach really do?
It opens the hair and takes the colour out. Then it leaves. Bleach Recovery goes in after and closes the door behind it.

FRINGE: One rule?
Wash less, condition more, and never trust a straightener at 230°. Your fringe will tell you everything else.
